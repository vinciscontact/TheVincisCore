About TheVincis

Engineering that wins customers, not applause.

TheVincis is a Chennai-based web design and development studio founded by Sathyanarayana D, a cybersecurity and IoT engineer. It builds websites, SaaS products, mobile apps and data systems for businesses that need technology to bring in customers, not just look good.

The founder

Sathyanarayana D

Founder & Principal Engineer

Sathyanarayana D founded TheVincis on one conviction: the tier-2 and tier-3 businesses that keep India running deserve the same engineering the metro giants pay millions for.

He trained in cybersecurity and IoT, is certified by Microsoft and Infosys, and has taught more than 200 professionals in corporate programs. Every TheVincis build carries that background: secure by default, and measured by what it brings in.

What we believe

  1. Working systems, not showpieces.

    If it won't bring you customers, we won't build it.

  2. You own everything.

    Domain, hosting, code and documentation, handed over in your name. No lock-in.

  3. Security is the default, not an upgrade.

    HTTPS, hardened forms and DevSecOps discipline on every build.

  4. Prove it with numbers.

    Every project is judged on leads, bookings, orders and time saved.

How we work

From first call to launch, in plain steps.

A business website typically ships in 2–4 weeks. Platforms and apps are scheduled milestone by milestone, always against a fixed written quote.

  1. 01

    Free consultation

    You tell us what the business is losing: leads, bookings, orders or time.

  2. 02

    Fixed written quote

    Scope, price and timeline agreed before any work begins.

  3. 03

    Design with live previews

    You see working pages as they are built, not static mock-ups.

  4. 04

    Build and search setup

    Fast pages, structured data and answer-first content for Google and AI assistants.

  5. 05

    Launch and handover

    Domain, hosting, code and documentation in your name, plus training for your team.
